Gaj se Square Feet nikalne ka Formula

Square Feet = Gaj × 9. Gaj asal me Square Yard hi hai — 1 yard = 3 feet, to 1 square yard = 3 × 3 = 9 square feet. Isliye ye conversion exact hai, approximate nahi.

Plot sizes Gaj me kyu boli jaati hain

Delhi-NCR, UP, Haryana, Rajasthan me residential plots ki size Gaj me chalti hai — "50 gaj ka makaan", "100 gaj ka plot", "200 gaj ki kothi". Builder aur naksha square feet me kaam karte hain, isliye Gaj ka 9 se multiply karna har property buyer ke kaam ka hisab hai.

100, 120, 250, 500 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet? (Seedha Jawab)

250 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet Hote Hain?

250 Gaj = 2,250 square feet (250 × 9) = 209.03 square meter. Aam plot size: 45 × 50 ft. Delhi-NCR me 250 gaj ki kothi ek badi residential property maani jaati hai.

100 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet Hote Hain?

100 Gaj = 900 square feet (100 × 9) = 83.61 square meter. Aam plot size: 30 × 30 ft, 25 × 36 ft ya 20 × 45 ft. Ye UP, Delhi-NCR aur Haryana me sabse common residential plot size hai.

120 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet Hote Hain?

120 Gaj = 1,080 square feet (120 × 9) = 100.34 square meter. Aam plot size: 30 × 36 ft. Colonies me 120 gaj ka plot bhi bahut chalta hai — 100 gaj se thoda bada.

500 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet Hote Hain?

500 Gaj = 4,500 square feet (500 × 9) = 418.06 square meter. Aam plot size: 60 × 75 ft. 500 gaj ka plot farmhouse, badi kothi ya commercial use ke liye hota hai.

50 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet Hote Hain?

50 Gaj = 450 square feet (50 × 9) = 41.81 square meter. Aam plot size: 15 × 30 ft. 50 gaj me aam taur par chhota makaan (ground + 1-2 floor) banta hai.

200 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet Hote Hain?

200 Gaj = 1,800 square feet (200 × 9) = 167.23 square meter. Aam plot size: 30 × 60 ft ya 40 × 45 ft. 200 gaj ka plot duplex ya bade independent ghar ke liye achha size hai.

150 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet Hote Hain?

150 Gaj = 1,350 square feet (150 × 9) = 125.42 square meter. Aam plot size: 30 × 45 ft. 150 gaj ka plot medium size ke ghar ke liye common hai.

300 Gaj Me Kitne Square Feet Hote Hain?

300 Gaj = 2,700 square feet (300 × 9) = 250.84 square meter. Aam plot size: 45 × 60 ft. 300 gaj ka plot badi kothi ya do-portion wale ghar ke liye hota hai.

Iska ulta bhi kaam aata hai — agar aapke paas square feet hai aur Gaj nikalna hai, to square feet ko 9 se divide kar do. Jaise 1,200 sq ft = 133.33 Gaj. Upar diye calculator me "Ulta convert" button daba kar ye seedha mil jaata hai.

Gaj, Varg Gaj aur Square Yard — Ek hi Cheez

Bahut log confuse hote hain ki "Gaj", "Varg Gaj" aur "Square Yard" alag hain — par ye teeno ek hi unit ke naam hain. 1 Gaj = 1 Varg Gaj = 1 Square Yard = 9 square feet. Hindi me "varg" ka matlab hi "square" hota hai. To agar kahin "varg gaj" likha ho, to use bilkul Gaj ki tarah hi 9 se multiply karein.

Gaj se Square Meter aur Bigha ka Hisab

Sirf square feet hi nahi, Gaj ko doosri units me bhi badla jaata hai:

  • Square Meter: Gaj × 0.8361 — jaise 100 Gaj = 83.61 sq meter
  • Square Feet: Gaj × 9
  • Bigha (UP Pucca): Gaj ÷ 3,025 — yani 100 Gaj = 0.033 Bigha

Registry aur sarkari kaagzaat me area aksar square meter me hota hai, jabki aam baat-cheet Gaj me — isliye dono ka hisab aana zaroori hai.

Gaj Plot ki Length aur Width Samajhna

Ek aur common sawaal — "100 Gaj plot ki length-width kitni hoti hai?" Yaad rahe, Gaj sirf area batata hai, shape nahi. 100 Gaj (900 sq ft) ka plot 30 ft × 30 ft ka square ho sakta hai, ya 45 ft × 20 ft ka rectangle — dono ka area 900 sq ft hi hai. Plot ki actual length-width naksha ya registry me alag se likhi hoti hai.
