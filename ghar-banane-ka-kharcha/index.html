Ye Kharcha Kaise Nikalta Hai

Formula seedha hai: Total built-up area (sq ft) × per sq ft rate. Agar aap gaj me daalte hain to pehle wo sq ft me badalta hai (1 gaj = 9 sq ft), phir floors se guna hota hai. Jaise 100 gaj = 900 sq ft; G+1 (do floor) = 1,800 sq ft built-up. Standard quality (~₹2,050/sq ft) pe ye lagbhag ₹36.9 lakh baithta hai.

Per sq ft rate me teen bade hisse hote hain — structure (RCC, dhalai, ent-gara) sabse bada (~56%), phir finishing (plaster, tiles, paint ~21%), aur services (electrical + plumbing ~12%). Baaki doors, windows waghera. Ismein zameen ki keemat, boundary wall aur furniture shaamil nahi — wo alag se jodein.

Sirf area convert karna ho to Gaj to Square Feet ya Gaj Plot Size tool use karein.

Frequently Asked Questions

100 gaj me ghar banane ka kharcha kitna aata hai?

100 gaj = 900 sq ft hota hai. Single floor (G) basic quality me lagbhag 12.6 se 15.3 lakh, standard me 16.2 se 20.7 lakh, aur premium me 22.5 se 28.8 lakh rupaye ka kharcha aata hai (2026 UP-Bihar rate). Do floor (G+1) banane par ye lagbhag double ho jaata hai. Ye estimate hai, exact quote local contractor se lein.

2026 me ghar banane ka rate per square feet kya hai?

2026 me UP-Bihar jaise tier-2 areas me basic construction lagbhag 1,400 se 1,700 rupaye per sq ft, standard 1,800 se 2,300 rupaye, aur premium 2,500 se 3,200 rupaye per sq ft aata hai. Metro cities me ye rate zyada hota hai.

Ghar banane ke kharche me kya kya shaamil hota hai?

Per sq ft rate me aam taur par structure (RCC, dhalai, brick work), finishing (plaster, tiles, paint), aur services (electrical + plumbing) shaamil hote hain. Ismein zameen ki keemat, boundary wall, aur interior/furniture shaamil nahi hote — wo alag se jodne padte hain.

Kya construction cost me contingency jodni chahiye?

Haan, total kharche ka 10 se 15 percent extra contingency ke liye rakhna chahiye, kyunki material rate badalte hain aur kaam ke dauran kuch extra kharche aa jaate hain.
