EMI Kaise Calculate Hoti Hai

EMI ka formula hai: EMI = P × R × (1+R)N / ((1+R)N − 1), jahan P loan amount hai, R monthly interest rate hai (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), aur N total months hain. Ye formula upar calculator me automatic lagta hai — aapko sirf 3 values daalni hain.

Plot/Zameen Loan Ka Interest Rate

Plot ya zameen loan ka interest rate aam taur par 8.5% se 12% ke beech hota hai — home loan se thoda zyada, kyunki banks plot loan ko zyada risky maante hain (construction guarantee nahi hota). Exact rate aapke bank, credit score aur loan-to-value ratio par depend karta hai. Zyada tar banks plot ki value ka 70-75% tak hi loan dete hain, baaki down payment khud dena padta hai.

Zameen Loan aur Home Loan Me Farak

Zameen/plot loan sirf khaali zameen kharidne ke liye hota hai, jabki home loan ghar banane ya ready ghar khareedne ke liye. Plot loan ka interest rate thoda zyada hota hai, aur income tax ke Section 80C/24 ka benefit tab hi milta hai jab plot par 3 saal ke andar construction ho jaaye. Sirf khaali plot rakhne par tax benefit seemit hote hain.

Frequently Asked Questions

5 lakh ke plot loan ki EMI kitni hogi?

5 lakh ke loan par, 10% interest rate aur 10 saal tenure me EMI lagbhag 6,608 rupaye monthly aayegi. Total interest lagbhag 2,93,000 rupaye banega. Rate aur tenure badalne par EMI badal jaati hai — upar calculator me apne numbers daalkar exact EMI dekhein.

Plot/land loan ka interest rate kitna hota hai?

Plot ya land loan ka interest rate aam taur par 8.5% se 12% ke beech hota hai, home loan se thoda zyada, kyunki bank plot loan ko zyada risky maante hain. Exact rate aapke bank, credit score aur loan amount par depend karta hai.

EMI calculate karne ka formula kya hai?

EMI ka formula hai: EMI = P x R x (1+R)^N / ((1+R)^N - 1), jahan P loan amount hai, R monthly interest rate hai (annual rate ko 12 se divide karke), aur N total months hai. Is calculator me ye formula automatic laga hota hai.

Zameen loan aur home loan me kya farak hai?

Zameen/plot loan sirf khaali zameen kharidne ke liye hota hai, jabki home loan ghar banane ya khareedne ke liye. Plot loan ka interest rate thoda zyada hota hai aur tax benefit bhi seemit hote hain, kyunki isme construction shaamil nahi hota.

Ye estimate hai. Exact EMI aapke bank ke processing fee, insurance aur exact interest rate par depend karegi — bank se confirm karein.
