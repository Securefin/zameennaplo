Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Ye policy batati hai ki zameennaplo.in ("Zameen Naplo", "hum") aapki jaankari ke saath kya karta hai.

1. Calculator data

Aap calculators me jo values daalte hain (area, price, loan amount wagaira) wo sirf aapke browser me process hoti hain. Ye data hamare server par na bheja jata hai, na store hota hai.

2. Analytics

Site ko behtar banane ke liye hum Google Analytics jaisi analytics services use kar sakte hain. Ye anonymous jaankari collect karti hain — jaise kaunsa page dekha gaya, device/browser type, aur approximate location (city/state level).

3. Cookies aur advertising

Hum Google AdSense ke zariye ads dikha sakte hain. Google aur uske partners cookies (DoubleClick cookie sahit) use karke aapke is site aur doosri sites ke visits ke adhaar par ads dikha sakte hain. Aap Google Ads Settings par personalised ads band kar sakte hain, ya aboutads.info par third-party cookies se opt-out kar sakte hain. Google partner sites ka data kaise use karta hai, ye yahan padhein.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — font load karne ke liye (aapka IP address Google tak jaata hai).
  • WhatsApp share — share button dabane par WhatsApp khulta hai; uske baad WhatsApp ki privacy policy lagu hoti hai.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.

5. Bachchon ki privacy

Ye site 13 saal se kam umar ke bachchon se jaan-boojhkar koi jaankari collect nahi karti.

6. Contact

Is policy ke baare me koi sawaal ho to Contact page par humse sampark karein.
