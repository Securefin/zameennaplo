Stamp Duty Kya Hota Hai Aur Kaise Calculate Hota Hai

Stamp duty ek sarkari tax hai jo property registry ke waqt lagta hai. Formula seedha hai: Stamp Duty = Property Value × State ka Rate (%). Iske upar Registration Charge alag se lagta hai (UP me 1%, Bihar me 2%). Dono milkar total registry kharcha banate hain.

Zaroori baat — stamp duty hamesha circle rate (sarkari guidance value) aur actual sale price, in dono me se jo zyada ho, uspar calculate hota hai. Agar aap circle rate se kam price par bhi deal karte hain, stamp duty circle rate par hi lagega.

Female Buyers Ko Kitni Chhoot Milti Hai

UP aur Bihar dono states me female buyers ko kam stamp duty rate milta hai — UP me 1% kam (7% se 6%, sirf ₹1 crore tak ki property par), Bihar me 0.6% kam (6.3% se 5.7%). 50 lakh ki property par UP me ye lagbhag 50,000 rupaye ki bachat hai. July 2025 me UP cabinet ne ye chhoot ki limit ₹10 lakh se badha kar ₹1 crore kar di. ₹1 crore se upar ki property par female buyer ko bhi poora 7% lagta hai — calculator ye automatically handle karta hai.

Registry Ke Aur Kharche

Stamp duty aur registration ke alawa registry me kuch chhote extra kharche bhi hote hain — dastavez (documentation) fee, vakil/lawyer charges, aur kabhi-kabhi mutation (dakhil-kharij) fee. Ye is calculator me shaamil nahi hain, lagbhag ₹5,000–15,000 extra maan ke chaliye.

Frequently Asked Questions

UP me stamp duty kitna percent hota hai?

UP me stamp duty male buyers ke liye 7% aur female buyers ke liye 6% hota hai (mahila chhoot sirf 1 crore tak ki property par). Joint (male-female) ownership ke liye yahan lagbhag 6.5% maana gaya hai. Registration charge sabke liye 1% alag se lagta hai.

Bihar me stamp duty kitna percent hota hai?

Bihar me stamp duty male buyers ke liye 6.3%, female buyers ke liye 5.7%, aur joint ownership ke liye lagbhag 6% hota hai. Registration charge alag se 2% lagta hai.

50 lakh ki property par UP me kitna stamp duty lagega?

50 lakh ki property par UP me male buyer ko 7% stamp duty (3.5 lakh) + 1% registration (50,000) = kul 4 lakh rupaye lagenge. Female buyer ko 6% stamp duty (3 lakh) + 1% registration (50,000) = kul 3.5 lakh rupaye lagenge.

Kya stamp duty circle rate par lagta hai ya sale price par?

Stamp duty hamesha circle rate (sarkari guidance value) aur actual sale price, in dono me se jo zyada ho, uspar calculate hota hai. Agar aap circle rate se kam par bhi kharidte hain, to stamp duty circle rate par hi lagega.

Rates 2026 ke public government notifications par based hain. Exact/latest rate apne Sub-Registrar office ya IGRS UP / Bhumijankari Bihar portal se confirm karein.
